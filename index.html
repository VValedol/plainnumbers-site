Plain Numbers

Plain Numbers is a small educational video channel: maps and charts built from open data, with AI narration. Every number is checked against public sources listed in each video description.

Channel: @plainnumbersmaps

Plain Numbers Uploader

“Plain Numbers Uploader” is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel’s own videos, thumbnails and captions to the Plain Numbers YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.

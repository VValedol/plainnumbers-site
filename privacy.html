Privacy Policy — Plain Numbers Uploader

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Plain Numbers Uploader (“the app”) is a private tool operated by the owner of the Plain Numbers channel. It is used only by that owner, with the owner’s own Google account.

What the app accesses

With the owner’s permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to: upload videos to the owner’s channel; set thumbnails and captions for those videos; read the list of the channel’s own uploads and their status, to avoid uploading the same video twice.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner’s computer. The app does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect data from any other users.

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored records can be deleted by the owner at any time.
